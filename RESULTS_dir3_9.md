# Results - directions 3,4,5,7,8,9 (preregistered in PREREG_dir3_9.md; code src/dir3_9.py; raw results/dir3_9.json). Not done: dir 6 (adenoma staging), dir 10 (functional pathways; GMM/KEGG tables not yet checked).
Baseline LOCO median AUROC 0.812 (dir 2).
Dir3 confound audit: study label is predictable from CONTROLS alone, accuracy 0.854 vs 0.154 majority (gate >2x: strong cohort signal = YES, cohort/batch effects are large). Per-cohort z-scoring raised LOCO median 0.812 -> 0.828 (+0.016; gate +0.02: NOT met), min 0.594 -> 0.630. Note the z-scoring uses unlabeled test-cohort statistics.
Dir4 minimal panel (top-k by |L1 coef| per training fold): k=5 median 0.776 (min 0.654), k=10 0.773, k=20 0.800 (min 0.585), k=50 0.799. Smallest k within 0.02 of full model: 20. A 5-species panel loses 0.036 median but has the best worst-case cohort (0.654).
Dir5 known taxa: all six (F. nucleatum s. animalis, Parvimonas micra, Peptostreptococcus stomatis, Gemella morbillorum, Solobacterium moorei, Porphyromonas asaccharolytica) higher in CRC in 9/9 cohorts; gate (>=7/9 same sign) met. This checks direction only; per-cohort Mann-Whitney p-values are in the json and several cohorts are not significant individually (e.g. US-CRC-2). Replication of published signal, not novel.
Dir7 transforms, LOCO median (min): log10 0.812 (0.594), CLR 0.802 (0.608), presence/absence 0.717 (0.595), raw relative abundance 0.679 (0.587). Log/CLR clearly better than raw.
Dir8 screening view (pooled LOCO predictions, no recalibration): specificity at 90% sensitivity ranges 0.036 (US-CRC-2) to 0.619 (AT); median ~0.46. Pooled Brier 0.189. Not screening-grade: at 90% sensitivity most cohorts lose over half of controls.
Dir9 covariates (age, sex, BMI; 8 cohorts, n=752 complete; IT-2 has none): covariates-only LOCO median 0.627, microbiome 0.833; gate (+0.03) met. US-CRC-2 is the exception (0.627 vs 0.585).
Limits: one model family, fixed C, species-level mOTUs2, CTR vs CRC only, no external non-Wirbel cohort. Nothing here is a new finding.
